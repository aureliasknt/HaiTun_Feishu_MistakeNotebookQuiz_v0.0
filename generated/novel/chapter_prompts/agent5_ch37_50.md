### Your Chapters

**Chapters 37-50 — "Choice of the Stars" (Part 3, the finale)**

- **Ch 37: 最后的信号 (The Last Signal)** — Using the last of their power, they send a distress call to the moving signal. A response comes. A ship decloaks — ancient, elegant, from a forgotten era. It saves them.
- **Ch 38: 静默者之船 (The Silent Ones' Ship)** — They board the ship. It's a time capsule from 300 years ago — automated, but carrying the complete historical record of the Silent Ones. An AI version of the original Seven speaks to them.
- **Ch 39: 封印的历史 (Sealed History)** — The ship's AI shows them the full truth: the Ether Drive was never a technology to be "repaired." It was a test. The Silent Ones wanted to see if humanity could evolve morally before they were given interstellar unity.
- **Ch 40: 代价 (The Price)** — The AI reveals the price of restarting the Ether Drive: it requires a consciousness to merge with the drive — a sacrifice. Not death, but transformation into something no longer fully human. The person who does this becomes the new "key."
- **Ch 41: 殊途 (Divergent Paths)** — Elena volunteers immediately — she believes it's her duty. Lu Chen argues against it, proposing a different solution: let the archives guide each isolated world to develop its own FTL, at its own pace. They argue passionately.
- **Ch 42: 苏婉的决定 (Su Wan's Decision)** — Unexpectedly, Su Wan speaks up. She offers a third option: she will enter the archive network as the new "steward" — not to restart the Ether Drive, but to repair and maintain the archive network so that knowledge survives for future generations when they ARE ready.
- **Ch 43: 高孟德的最后一击 (Gao's Final Strike)** — Before they can act, Gao Mengde's ship arrives, having tracked them. He boards the Silent Ones' ship with an armed squad. A confrontation.
- **Ch 44: 三方会谈 (Three-Way Parley)** — Gao, Lu Chen, Elena, and Su Wan face each other. Each represents a different vision for humanity's future. Gao: unity through strength. Elena: unity through technology. Lu Chen: cautious progress. Su Wan: preservation and patience.
- **Ch 45: 致命失误 (Fatal Error)** — Gao, impatient, tries to force the AI to give him the restart codes. His brute-force approach damages the ship's core systems. A chain reaction begins that could destroy everything — the ship, the data, all of them.
- **Ch 46: 牺牲 (Sacrifice)** — Hé, the AI that has guided them through everything, makes its choice. It declares that its 400-year wait is over — it will merge with the damaged core to stabilize it. Hé's final words to Lu Chen: "I was created to serve humanity's knowledge. This is the most meaningful service I can perform."
- **Ch 47: 告别鹤 (Farewell to Hé)** — Emotional farewell as Hé sacrifices itself to stabilize the Silent Ones' ship and protect the archive. The ship is saved, but Hé is gone. The data is intact but the choice about the Ether Drive remains.
- **Ch 48: 第三条路 (The Third Path)** — Inspired by Hé's sacrifice, they find a compromise: the Silent Ones' ship becomes a mobile archive that travels between star systems, sharing knowledge but not the FTL technology. Each system develops at its own pace. Su Wan volunteers to stay as the steward.
- **Ch 49: 散场 (Departure)** — Farewells. Elena returns to the Federation remnants, carrying a message of patience. Gao, humbled, returns to his system, now knowing that conquest without wisdom is hollow. Lu Chen and Su Wan part — her to the Silent Ship, him to continue his work as an archivist.
- **Ch 50: 星辰有光 (The Stars Still Shine)** — Epilogue, one year later. Lu Chen is rebuilding the archive on Tianshu-7. He receives a message from Su Wan — the ship has visited three systems, sharing knowledge. A new generation of scientists, inspired by ancient wisdom, is making breakthroughs — their FTL, their pace. The stars still shine. The end is not an ending but a beginning.
