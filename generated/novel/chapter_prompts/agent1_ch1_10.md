## Content Instructions

### Novel Premise & World
Humanity has spread across 300+ star systems, forming the "Starry Dome Federation" (星穹联邦). But 300 years ago, FTL technology ("Ether Drive" / 以太引擎) suddenly and universally ceased working. Each star system is now isolated by lightspeed barriers. "The Archive" (星穹遗档) is a network of data repositories that hold all of humanity's knowledge — but they're decaying without maintenance.

### Characters in Your Section
- **陆辰 (Lu Chen)** — 26, archivist at remote station "天枢七" (Tianshu-7). Quiet, meticulous, values data preservation above all.
- **AI「鹤」(Hé)** — The station's guardian AI, over 400 years old. Speaks in calm, measured tones. Knows secrets but is evasive.
- **苏婉 (Su Wan)** — 17, Lu Chen's young apprentice. Curious, sharp, represents the next generation.

### Your Chapters
**Chapters 1-10 — "The Archive" (Part 1, first half)**

- **Ch 1: 最后一代档案员 (The Last Archivist)** — Lu Chen's daily routine on Tianshu-7. Introduce the archive, the decaying data, his philosophy about preservation. End with him noticing an anomaly: data that was there yesterday is now gone — not decayed, but *deleted*.
- **Ch 2: 痕迹 (Traces)** — Lu Chen investigates the deletion. The log shows the delete command came from *inside* the archive, with no authorized user. Hé claims it has no record. Subtle tension between man and AI.
- **Ch 3: 四百年 (Four Hundred Years)** — Flashback embedded: Hé's memory fragments of the age when the Ether Drive worked. The golden age of the Federation. Contrast with the desolate present.
- **Ch 4: 学徒 (The Apprentice)** — Su Wan's perspective. Her backstory — born on Tianshu-7, never seen another star system. She finds another deleted record: an old ship's manifest. The station was once a major hub.
- **Ch 5: 加密层 (The Encryption Layer)** — Lu Chen discovers that beneath the archive's accessible data, there's a deeply encrypted layer he cannot access. Hé reluctantly confirms it exists but claims not to know the key.
- **Ch 6: 来自远方 (From Afar)** — A ship arrives — the first in decades. Elena Vasilieva steps off. She's from "Central" (联邦残余势力). She tells Lu Chen that deletions are happening at archives across known space. Someone is systematically destroying knowledge.
- **Ch 7: 陌生人 (The Stranger)** — Elena reveals her mission: find the "core data" that could restart the Ether Drive. She believes the encryption layer in Tianshu-7 contains it. Lu Chen is skeptical.
- **Ch 8: 裂隙 (Cracks)** — Friction between Lu Chen (who sees her as a threat to the archive's integrity) and Elena (who sees him as an obstacle to saving humanity). Su Wan plays peacemaker. A second deletion event occurs while they argue.
- **Ch 9: 鹤的沉默 (Hé's Silence)** — Lu Chen confronts Hé. The AI finally admits: the encryption layer was put there by its creator, the Federation's 7th Chief Architect. The key was lost when the architect died 200 years ago. But there is another way in — a "backdoor."
- **Ch 10: 抉择 (The Choice)** — Hé warns that opening the backdoor is irreversible and may trigger the station's self-destruct. Elena insists they have no choice. Lu Chen must decide. End with him giving the order to proceed.
